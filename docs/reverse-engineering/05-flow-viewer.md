# 05 · Flow viewer

| Claim | Label |
|---|---|
| Flows are a browsable facet for apps (`/explore/web/flows/{slug}`) with action-style names ("logging in", "filtering & sorting") | Seen |
| Sites have no flows facet (sections + styles instead) | Seen |
| How a flow is stepped through (strip, autoplay, captions) | Unknown — the flow player is behind login |

## Our decision
Our own flow player: horizontal strip of numbered steps + large preview, ←/→ and 1–9 to jump,
optional caption per step, autoplay toggle (2.5 s per step), respects reduced motion.
